# Brand Reference Classification

## Purpose

The Brand Book and logo asset archive are supplied as reference evidence for Micro identity. They are not an automatic authorization to use every logo color as a product UI token.

## Allowed identity roles

- `#D97757`: Micro identity/create/FAB role already approved in Standard.
- `#C96442`: chosen/current/pressed edge and underline already approved in Standard.
- `#141413`: Warm-Ink value/commitment/justified-danger role already approved in Standard.
- The official logo files remain unchanged and are not recolored by financial state.

## Restricted or rejected translations

- `#B79C86` and `#8C7A66` are logo-only neutrals. Do not use them as product text, borders, surfaces, or buttons.
- Turquoise from the logo is not a success/failure pair and must not be used to encode financial direction. No new Turquoise UI role is approved in this execution.
- Do not introduce `#964E33` or `#5F3120` as new Standard tokens or primary buttons.
- Dark logo assets do not authorize Dark Mode. Standard remains Light-only.
- Logo motion assets do not become financial loading or status indicators.
- The logo forms are not module icons, product-area markers, account markers, or financial states.

## Approved product UI decision

For ordinary save/confirm actions, use the existing warm tint `#F5F4ED` with dark text/icon and a `#C96442` pressed edge. Do not use a filled `#D97757` button for ordinary save/confirm, and do not use a filled `#141413` button unless the action is a clear commitment or high-consequence confirmation.

## Source boundary

Use the Brand Book and asset archive to preserve logo construction, official variants, and identity fidelity. Use `OWNER_APPROVED_DECISIONS.md` for product UI roles. If the two levels appear to conflict, preserve the product UI decision and stop for an owner decision rather than improvising.
