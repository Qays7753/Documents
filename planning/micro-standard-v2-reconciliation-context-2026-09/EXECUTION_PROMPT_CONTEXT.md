## Unified decisions to implement

### A. Keep these Standard foundations unchanged

Do not reopen or replace the current approved foundation:

- Warm light canvas and surfaces.
- `#D97757` for identity/create/FAB identity role.
- `#C96442` for chosen/current/pressed edge and underline roles.
- `#141413` for high-consequence commitment and dangerous actions.
- Warm Tint ordinary save/confirm class.
- Semantic colors for semantic outcomes, never for identity substitution.
- Light-first Standard; no Dark Mode tokens in this package.
- Arabic-first RTL, English digits, bidi isolation, `د.أ`/`دأ` outside numeric values.
- Current spacing, radius, elevation, geometry, motion, accessibility, and no-new-palette rules.
- Existing honest-void contract: unrecorded, unavailable, and measured zero remain distinct.
- Prototype is evidence only; it is never a source of product copy, routes, data, or financial meaning.

Do not reintroduce `#964E33`, `#5F3120`, gold/amber, steel blue, retired palette restrictions, or any new color value.

### B. General-purpose contract additions allowed in the 29-file Standard

Add or clarify only the following reusable contracts:

1. **Knowledge-state presentation contract:** a general presentation contract for unconfirmed, unknown, incomplete, needs-review, and estimated information. It must define word/marker/tone behavior and must not impose Micro-specific vocabulary or business meaning.
2. **Operational-row marker contract:** word plus non-color marker, with an optional inline-start edge stripe no thicker than 3px and never relying on color alone.
3. **AUX behavior addendum:** route-kind chrome behavior, keyboard-driven chrome hiding, safe-area clearance, context-label suppression, scroll-border behavior, and route transition guidance. This is a visual/interaction contract, not a route list.
4. **Period-control variants:** support both a period chip and a native month/date-input variant. Time semantics, default period, and product-specific ranges remain outside the Standard.
5. **Quiet feedback variant:** document inline/quiet completion and result feedback as a valid alternative to Snackbar. Do not make Snackbar mandatory.
6. **Overlay versus in-flow guidance:** consequential confirmation and deletion use Dialog/Sheet; continuous explanation and editing can remain in-flow. Do not convert every panel into an overlay.
7. **Typography floor clarification:** 13px minimum for labels; 12px only for non-financial metadata; financial facts and amounts never below 15px.
8. **Icon and RTL adapter guidance:** define mirror flags and semantic icon roles without forcing a specific production icon library.
9. **Authority ladder:** Standard = visual contracts; Micro mapping = runtime carrier; Micro docs = implementation guidance; domain/application/storage = product meaning and persistence.
10. **Verification and manifest corrections:** make the 29-core + 2-metadata split explicit and preserve the exact testing limitations. Do not claim device or screen-reader testing.

### C. Do not move these into the Standard core

Keep the following as Micro-owned patterns or product decisions:

- Current Micro state words and Arabic product vocabulary.
- Micro route names and navigation labels.
- Finance formulas, posting, reversal, sync, permissions, storage, and domain policy.
- Correction lifecycle, decision cards, fact-state triad, integrity-check presentation, scheduling capacity/recurrence, party ledger, and other Micro-specific feature patterns.
- Tables, calendar implementation, charts questions, sort policy, order-detail data anatomy, and tool-result business meaning.
- QuickActionSheet sale/expense behavior and its financial forms.
- Dark Mode tokens, Dark Mode implementation, and Dark Mode activation.
- AI Assistant, LLM, chat, chatbot, or any related feature.

You may document that these patterns are valid consumers of the Standard contracts, but do not encode their product meaning into the 29 files.
