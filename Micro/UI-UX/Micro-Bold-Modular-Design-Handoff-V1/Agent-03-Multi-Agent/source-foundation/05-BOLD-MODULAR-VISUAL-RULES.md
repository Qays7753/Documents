# Bold Modular Visual Rules

## 1. Visual energy by product area

| Area | Expression level | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| مشروعي الآن | High | Strong status composition, controlled color field, clear action energy. |
| العمل والطلبات | High | Expressive state and progress, strong differentiation, visible exceptions. |
| Recording flows | High/focused | Dominant amount, clear choices, immediate feedback, minimal distraction. |
| Success | High/brief | Strong but short confirmation and visible impact. |
| المالية | Medium | Numeric discipline, semantic color, clean surfaces, traceable totals. |
| أدواتي | Medium-low | Modular grouping with restrained expression. |
| Records and settings | Low | Quiet, dense, searchable, and highly legible. |
| السوق future | Highest potential | May carry the boldest identity after real product scope exists. |

## 2. Color architecture

Every direction must propose values for the roles below and state where each may and may not be used.

| Role | Purpose | Prohibited misuse |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Warm | Identity, create/start actions, selected brand moments | Profit, generic success, error |
| Trust Blue | Information, active context, navigation, system confidence | Positive money unless explicitly informational |
| Positive Green | Complete positive financial result | Generic saved/success toast by default |
| Attention Amber | Incomplete, attention, pending review | Decoration or normal navigation |
| Negative Red | Complete negative result, danger, destructive error | Ordinary expenses without context |
| Neutral Canvas | Page environment and breathing space | Full beige monotony |
| Clean Surface | Forms, records, analysis, critical reading | Making every section a white card |
| Dark Ink | Text, figures, strong commitment actions | Overuse as heavy black blocks |

### Contrast requirements

- Normal text: at least 4.5:1.
- Large text: at least 3:1.
- Informative icons and control boundaries: at least 3:1 where required.
- Never round a failing ratio into a pass.
- Show foreground, background, use, and ratio for every proposed core pair.

## 3. Color-block rules

A strong color field is allowed only when it performs at least one function:

1. Defines a major zone.
2. Announces status.
3. Directs attention.
4. Supports an action.
5. Shows progress or change.
6. Creates recognizable product identity.

Rules:

- One dominant focal field per viewport.
- Supporting color fields must be quieter than the dominant field.
- Do not assign unrelated rainbow colors to every card.
- Use saturated backgrounds sparingly around financial text.
- A colored surface must have a defined light and dark mode equivalent.
- Gradients are optional experiments, never the identity foundation; include a solid alternative.

## 4. Surface hierarchy

Use a maximum of four purposeful levels:

1. Canvas.
2. Group/section ground.
3. Reading surface.
4. Overlay/sheet/dialog.

Avoid card-inside-card structures. A group may be open with dividers instead of a rounded container. Shadows are reserved for overlays and real elevation; borders and tonal separation handle most layout structure.

## 5. Typography

### Foundation

- Start with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic UI and IBM Plex Mono/tabular numerals for financial values.
- The designer may propose one alternative Arabic family only if it materially improves the direction and includes licensing, loading, and readability rationale.

### Hierarchy requirements

- One hero answer per screen.
- Card title must not equal body text in size and apparent weight.
- Body text should remain comfortable at common mobile density.
- Critical explanatory text must not be 12px by default.
- Arabic letter spacing is not used as a decorative technique.
- Currency and numeric runs remain stable and isolated in RTL.

The designer must supply proposed sizes, line heights, weights, and examples with long Arabic strings.

## 6. Iconography

- Use one icon family per direction.
- Utility icons may remain outline-based.
- Active navigation and primary actions require stronger expression through weight, container, or controlled fill.
- Do not mix emoji, filled illustrations, and thin outline icons.
- Mirror only directional icons.
- Test culturally ambiguous actions with visible labels.

Required concepts: بيع، مصروف، طلب، تحصيل، الكاش، الدين، النتيجة، التكلفة، محفظة، مورد، موعد، تسليم.

## 7. Components to explore

- Business Status Hero.
- Micro Signal.
- Quick Action Bar.
- Financial Result.
- Metric Group/Row.
- Attention Item.
- Order State/Progress.
- Operational Row.
- Impact Preview.

These are concept components, not final production components.

## 8. Motion

Motion communicates causality and state change.

### Required success sequence

1. Immediate press feedback.
2. Save/progress state that prevents accidental duplicate action.
3. Success confirmation with amount and destination.
4. Transition back to context.
5. Brief highlight of the affected value.

### Prohibited motion

- Confetti.
- Count-up animation that delays a financial answer.
- Repeating pulse.
- Bounce-heavy transitions.
- Parallax.
- Motion that survives reduced-motion preference.

## 9. Light and dark modes

- Light mode is the primary concept presentation.
- Each direction includes at least one dark-mode home screen and a dark palette rationale.
- Dark mode is not a literal inversion.
- Saturated colors must be adjusted for glare and text contrast.
- Brand and semantic roles remain distinguishable in both modes.

## 10. Accessibility and RTL gate

- Primary controls target 44–48px as a Micro usability contract.
- No essential target may be obscured by bottom navigation or safe-area content.
- State is never color-only.
- Visible focus treatment is specified.
- Text zoom to 200% preserves the task.
- Form labels remain visible.
- Error text explains problem and recovery.
- Dialogs and sheets define focus, close, and return behavior.
- Mixed Arabic, Latin numbers, currency, phone, date, and IDs are demonstrated.

## 11. Anti-patterns

Reject a concept that depends on:

- Equal card grids.
- Multiple competing hero metrics.
- Beige-on-beige subtlety.
- Generic fintech blue dominance.
- Random module colors.
- Decorative dashboards.
- Tiny pastel text.
- Icon-only critical actions.
- Visual intensity that hides financial meaning.
- Imitation of Shopify, Stripe, Salla, Notion, or another single product.
