# Direction Evaluation Rubric

## Scoring method

Score each criterion from 1 to 5 and multiply by its weight. Scores are expert estimates until validated with users. A high total does not override a critical rejection condition.

| Criterion | Weight | Evaluation question |
|---|---:|---|
| Five-second financial understanding | 18% | Can the owner correctly describe the current business state? |
| Action discoverability and speed | 14% | Is the most useful next action visible and understandable? |
| Sense of control | 12% | Does the screen reduce uncertainty and show what to do? |
| Trust in figures | 12% | Are result, completeness, period, and source credible? |
| Visual vitality | 10% | Does the product feel awake and responsive without noise? |
| Comfort and sustained use | 8% | Can finance and records be read for longer sessions? |
| Low-experience usability | 8% | Can a non-accountant understand labels and interactions? |
| Distinct Micro identity | 7% | Is the direction recognizable without logo imitation? |
| Arabic RTL quality | 4% | Are hierarchy, mixed numbers, icons, and flow genuinely RTL? |
| Accessibility | 4% | Do contrast, targets, text scaling, and status cues pass? |
| Future scalability | 3% | Can the system extend to market, delivery, and cloud states? |

## Score anchors

### 1 — Fails

The direction actively obstructs the criterion or relies on an invalid assumption.

### 2 — Weak

The criterion exists but requires explanation or causes frequent hesitation.

### 3 — Acceptable

The criterion works for a typical case but has visible gaps.

### 4 — Strong

The criterion is clear, repeatable, and appropriate for the primary user.

### 5 — Excellent

The criterion is immediate, distinctive, accessible, and robust across states.

## Mandatory gates

A direction cannot win if any of these fail:

- Financial truth.
- Primary action visibility.
- Arabic RTL integrity.
- Normal-text contrast.
- Incomplete-result comprehension.
- Critical status with non-color cue.
- No bottom-navigation occlusion.

## Expert review outputs

For each direction provide:

- Weighted score.
- Evidence for each score.
- Top three strengths.
- Top three risks.
- Fixable weaknesses.
- Structural weaknesses.
- User-test questions.
- Final recommendation: keep, revise, or reject.

## User-test checks

Use tasks, not preference alone:

1. “احكيلي كيف وضع المشروع اليوم.”
2. “هل النتيجة محسوبة بشكل كامل؟”
3. “سجّل بيعًا بقيمة 25 دينارًا.”
4. “وين راح المبلغ بعد التسجيل؟”
5. “مين عليه مبلغ للمشروع؟”
6. “شو الطلب اللي يحتاج انتباهك؟”

Record:

- Task success.
- Time to first correct interpretation.
- Misclicks and hesitation.
- Incorrect financial interpretation.
- Perceived control.
- Trust.
- Visual comfort.
- Perceived vitality.

Preference may break a tie, but cannot override task failure.
