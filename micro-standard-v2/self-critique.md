# Self-Critique

The system is intentionally restrained. Its next quality gains should come from Micro domain compositions, better financial state language, simple question-led charts, and complete operational flows—not from adding more colors or decorative cards.

## Post-run critique (run-20260913-msv2-zai-01)

What improved: the contracts are now executable (named action classes, state presentation matrix, value-zone and row slots), the shipped tokens match what the gallery consumes, the selection grammar matches what the documents claim, and the gallery demonstrates every contract with honest states (zero, no-data, unknown, pending) instead of decorative fills.

What remains deliberately restrained: no new palette, no dark mode, no chart library, no product policy. Two disclosed alpha derivatives (scrim, translucent header) are the only non-approved-value composites, and they exist because a required scrim was invisible without them.

Honest limitations: the filled warm-ink surface is now rare by design, so screens that over-use it would violate the package — Micro compositions must adopt the save/create classes rather than reaching for the ink fill. State words, tags, navigation and segment labels render at the 13px label step (the clarified type floor); the 12px caption floor now carries non-financial metadata only, and financial words never go below 15px. Verification evidence is headless-browser only; device and screen-reader checks remain separate release gates. The value/period/row slots are neutral contracts — their meaningful use depends on Micro product composition, not on this package.

## Final Copy reconciliation critique

The independent audit found one concrete contract defect in the upstream run: a text-bearing Create button used white on Clay at 3.12:1 even though the documentation restricted Clay to non-text use. The Final Copy corrects this by separating text ink from icon-only ink; the identity color and all approved palette values remain unchanged.

The audit also found stale delivery metadata: the run manifest pointed to an older head and understated the run-folder count. The Final Copy rewrites the handoff records to identify the source head separately and keeps publication status explicit until `main` is verified.

The Prototype is intentionally not promoted to a source of truth. It remains a visual evidence artifact; its illustrative words, numbers, and scene composition must not become Micro product behavior. The next gate is a read-only Micro composition and architecture scan before any structural implementation.

## Reconciliation run critique (run-20260914-msv2-reconciliation-01)

What improved: the package now covers the knowledge-state tier (presentation only), the AUX behavior contract, both period-control variants, quiet inline feedback, overlay-vs-in-flow guidance, the clarified type floor, icon adapter guidance, the authority ladder, and the explicit 29+2 manifest split — all traceable to the unified decision register, with no new palette, timing, or geometry value.

What was corrected: the gallery previously contradicted its own accessibility rule in one defect class — tag words, completion words, delta lines, and row amounts were set in semantic hues that fail text contrast on their surfaces, and success/status markers sat on grounds where they fall below the 3:1 non-text minimum. Words now render in text-safe inks with the semantic hue on the leading marker (success/status markers on the white Surface); amounts render in ink with the sign as the non-color direction marker; the bottom navigation gains safe-area clearance. These are rebindings of approved values — nothing was added to the palette.

What remains deliberately restrained: no new palette, no dark mode, no chart library, no product policy, no Micro vocabulary fixed as policy (knowledge-state words stay examples). The knowledge tier is presentation-only — deciding which data is unconfirmed, incomplete, or estimated remains product-owned. Verification evidence remains headless-browser only; device and screen-reader checks remain separate release gates.
