Design evidence

Identity & accent ramps

The current Standard uses warm ink for one text-bearing commitment action, neutral tint for secondary action, and Clay for identity/create. Clay is never used as ordinary body-size text; chosen/current is a 2px edge plus a non-color cue.

Semantic pairings

Semantic colors are sparse outcome marks. They must be paired with words and shapes; charts use semantic marks only when they answer a clear question. The current Standard does not use the old gold ramp as a required identity family.
