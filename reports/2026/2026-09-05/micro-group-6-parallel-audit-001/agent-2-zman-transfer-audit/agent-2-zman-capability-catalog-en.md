## 1. Method and independence rules

The Zman catalogue below was rebuilt from Zman source code by SA-1 (Zman Capability Archaeologist), independently of the prior gap-analysis report (`zaman-to-micro-gap-analysis-001`). The prior report's 24 capabilities (ZC-01…ZC-24) were each re-verified against code: 21 CONFIRMED as described, 3 CONFIRMED-WITH-DIFFERENCES (ZC-01 comment drift, ZC-06 forfeit source, ZC-17 soft-not-hard delete), and 6 additional capabilities discovered (ZC-25…ZC-30). Every `file:line` citation in this catalog refers to Zman @ `bdd63ab` or Micro @ `961051f` unless prefixed `Z ` / `M `. Deep dossiers per capability (problem, entry, steps, inputs, validation, storage, derived values, effects, unknown/zero behavior, correction, offline, completion, discoverability) are in `agent-2-specialist-01-zman-archaeology-en.md` §3.

Two corrections to the audit brief itself, verified in code (recorded for the owner, no code impact):

- **The brief's "project relationship and shared allocation by amount, percentage, estimate, or deferment" does NOT exist in Zman.** Exhaustive search over Zman source and all 29 migrations found no project entity, no allocation column, and no shared-expense split UI; all `مشروع` matches are generic copy and all `توزيع` matches are single-asset depreciation spread or read-only analytics. Shared-expense allocation is a **Micro-only capability** (`OperatingExpenseContext.sharedExpense` with amount/percentage/defer modes, `src/domain/financial-event/types.ts`, `calculateSharedProjectShareMinor`). Micro is stronger here; this is a reverse gap, not a Micro gap.
- **The brief's "INV-4: sale posts max(0, amount − deposit)" does not match Zman code.** Zman books full realized revenue and nets on the cash side (deposit transform + remainder movement, `Z finance/actions.ts:1528-1595`). Micro's model (revenue = full price at delivery; cash = collections) is equivalent-or-stricter. (SA-4 finding F-9.)
