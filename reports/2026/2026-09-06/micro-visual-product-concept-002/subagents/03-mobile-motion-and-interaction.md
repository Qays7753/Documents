## 1. Motion principles for Micro — the eight meanings

Every animation in Micro must carry one of eight meanings. If a proposed motion carries none of them, it is decoration and is rejected outright (C-17: differentiation through interaction, never ornament).

**1. Continuity.** The owner never loses their place. Transient surfaces close over the standing screen — a receipt closes over the Home screen that never moved; a sheet dims it rather than replacing it. Tab switches and reader returns restore the exact scroll position and row the owner left. Motion says: *this is the same world; exactly one thing changed* (the anti-pattern register: "silent saves" and "lost input" are continuity failures).

**2. Location.** Motion tells where something came from and where it went. Sheets rise from the bottom edge where the FAB «سجّل» lives (the recorder feels like it originates at the recorder). Pushed readers arrive from the reading-direction end edge. The active digit position is always visible on the NumericSurface. Any transition that cannot answer "from where, to where" is cut.

**3. Focus.** One mover per event. When the digit position changes, only the underline moves. When a Finance layer expands, content below translates as one block and content above never moves. When a save completes, exactly one mark appears. Attention is spent once, deliberately — never on two simultaneous moving elements (this protects the "one primary action per screen" rule C-14 from motion-side competition).

**4. Saving.** The write is visible at the point of commitment — the button — and nowhere else. The button swaps to «جارٍ التسجيل…» with a small inline arc and becomes inert; the rest of the screen never dims, never blocks, never pretends to be unavailable. Saving is a fact about the button, not a fact about the screen.

**5. Completion.** Success is a sentence, not a celebration. The receipt replaces the form in place inside the sheet; a calm confirmation mark scales in with zero overshoot; the closure sentence carries final numbers; then stillness. No confetti, no bounce, no counting digits (12-state vocabulary, C-12: success = non-blocking closure sentence).

**6. Waiting.** Where truth does not exist yet, an honest placeholder shaped like the real layout holds the place, and text carries the truth («جارٍ قراءة تقديراتك…»). Where content already exists, it stays visible — nothing ever spins over known content. Waiting never manufactures motion to look busy (C-06, C-11, C-12).

**7. Risk.** Before a destructive or high-impact action, motion stops. CorrectionPreview's two lists («ما سيتغير / ما لن يتغيّر») are static — no item stagger, no icons drawing themselves. The confirm button stays gated until the reason rule passes. Destructive dialogs do not dismiss on scrim tap. Deliberate stillness is the visual grammar of "read this before you commit."

**8. Recovery.** Errors are inline, non-destructive, and reversible in feel: the typed input stays on screen, the error text appears in a reserved slot (no layout shift), retry sits at hand. Nothing shakes — a shaking field is motion as punishment, and Micro does not punish the owner for the product's or the world's failure.

**Three global rules that follow from these:**

- **First paint is settled.** Screens render in their resting state on load — no entrance animations. Motion responds only to a user action or to a state change caused by a completed action (skeletons for genuinely unknown content are the truthful exception; deep-linked `?layer=` / `?focus=` expansion renders already-expanded, without animation).
- **One codebase, two directions.** Page-level motion (slides, chevron sides, back-button direction, tab travel) mirrors with `dir`; LTR-isolated money and date runs (`bdi`, C-09/C-10) never mirror internally and never re-flow during transitions. Vertical, scale, and fade patterns are direction-neutral and identical in both modes.
- **Money numerals never animate individually.** Amounts ride their containers; they are always rendered as final tabular ASCII digits with `en-US` grouping. No counting, no digit scatter, no per-glyph motion.

**The ONE allowed localized highlight.** Exactly one use of a localized background highlight exists in the whole product: after a write completes, the single figure on the standing screen that the write changed (e.g., «الكاش المسجل 145.00 → 165.00 د.أ») receives a one-time tint — light `#e3f5f5` (accent-soft) → transparent; dark `#8fd5d6` at 12% alpha → transparent — fading over 600ms, `M-flat`, fired once per write, never looping, never on mere navigation or first paint. It clarifies a meaningful change; it never replaces text, icons, or the accessible announcement — the closure sentence and `aria-live` fire whether or not the highlight renders. In reduced motion it is omitted entirely; the closure sentence carries the change. No other highlight exists anywhere in Micro.

## 2. Pattern specifications

Curve names (defined fully in §3): **M-out** `cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)` (enter/expand, zero overshoot) · **M-in** `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0.8, 0.15)` (exit/collapse) · **M-flat** `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (state changes, fades) · **linear** (continuous loops only). All alpha values below are tints of fixed palette roles — no new hues are created.

### 2.3 NumericSurface — amount entry

The surface renders the amount as a **fixed LTR slot grid**: 6 integer slots + 2 decimal slots with pre-rendered separators (up to 999,999.99), the unit «د.أ» anchored after the number in logical order (visually to the left in RTL), tabular ASCII digits, `en-US` grouping. Leading unfilled slots show quiet placeholders at 32% opacity. The grid, dot, separators, unit, labels, and keypad never move during entry — the frame is the stability contract ("no reflow/jump").

## 3. Global timing and easing scale

| Class | Duration | Applies to | Curve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | 80–120ms | Press feedback, focus ring, chevron/indicator rotation, glyph fade, underline slide, single-slot digit shift | **M-flat** `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` |
| Standard | 150–220ms | Cross-fades, disclosure height, dialogs, scrim, error fade, guard panel, content swaps inside a stable frame, block translate, sheet close | Fades M-flat; growth M-out; shrink M-in |
| Surface | 240–280ms | Sheet rise, reader/deep-editor push and pop, sheet keyboard re-anchor, large-surface height settle | Enter **M-out**; exit **M-in** |
| Continuous loops | 1200ms | Sync arc rotation; skeleton opacity pulse | linear (the only linear uses) |
| Hold-release | 600ms | The one localized highlight's fade to transparent | M-flat |

**Named curves:** M-out `cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)` — decelerating entrance, zero overshoot; M-in `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0.8, 0.15)` — accelerating exit; M-flat `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` — short state changes.

**Choosing rule:** pick the duration class by the *role* of the change — feedback (micro), content replacement inside a stable frame (standard), whole-surface travel or resize (surface). Pick the curve by *role* too: entering/expanding = M-out, exiting/collapsing = M-in, everything else = M-flat; an exit never uses an entrance curve. When two elements move in one event they share one curve, and their durations differ by at most one class step. When in doubt, choose the shorter duration: an unread animation is a wasted animation, and Micro is calm. **Exceptions beyond 280ms of travel are forbidden; the only non-transition durations allowed are the two continuous loops (1200ms, a state not a transition) and the 600ms highlight hold-release (a static tint whose removal fades — no travel).** OS keyboard timing is system-owned and never overridden.

## 4. Quiet-completion choreography — quick sale, ms by ms

Context: Home standing screen; FAB → QuickActionSheet → «تسجيل بيع»; amount 20.00 typed (wallet «الدرج», no options); write = `directSales.record` + allocation, idempotency-keyed, local-first (offline writes succeed identically — verified).

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| **T0** | Tap «سجّل البيع». Button pressed role (100ms, `#b4613f` light / dark overlay); idempotency key minted. |
| **T0+80ms** | Saving state: label crossfades to «جارٍ التسجيل…» (150ms, M-flat), 16dp inline arc (1200ms/rev linear), button width reserved, `aria-busy`; form fields inert but not dimmed; **nothing else on the screen changes**. |
| **T_W** (typically T0+100–300ms) | Write commits atomically. If the write takes >600ms, nothing new animates — the button stays truthful. On failure, jump to the last row of this table. |
| **T_W+0–120ms** | Form fades out (fields, custom keypad, options row) — M-in; keypad fades with the form. Scrim and sheet frame unchanged. |
| **T_W+120–320ms** | Receipt renders; sheet height settles to the receipt's height (200ms, M-out) — bottom-anchored, so the top edge lowers calmly with no jump; confirmation mark scales 0.8→1 + fades in (200ms, M-out, **zero overshoot**). |
| **T_W+180–330ms** | Closure sentence fades in (150ms, M-flat): «سُجّل بيع 20.00 د.أ — الكاش زاد 20.00 وصار 165.00 د.أ». Numbers render as final tabular ASCII — **never counted up**. |
| **T_W+270–420ms** | Action row fades in (150ms, M-flat): [افتح السجل → /direct-sales/:id] · [تراجع → CorrectionPreview] · [تم]. |
| **T_W+420ms** | Chain complete. Focus moves to the receipt container (tabindex=-1) — screen readers read the closure sentence; a polite live region duplicates it. Then **settle**: nothing further moves. No auto-dismiss; the owner closes with «تم». |
| **T_W+420–1020ms** | Beneath the scrim, the changed cash figure on Home receives the ONE localized highlight: `#e3f5f5` → transparent (600ms, M-flat, once). |
| **«تم» tap** | Sheet slides down (220ms, M-in); scrim fades (150ms); FAB returns (fade + scale, 150ms). Standing screen proves continuity — it never moved. |
| **Failure at any point** | Button returns to «سجّل البيع» (150ms); inline error in the reserved slot: «ما انحفظ البيع — الرقم باقي مكتوب، جرّب مرة ثانية» (assertive, `aria-describedby`); **the amount stays on screen** — nothing lost, nothing navigates. |

**Reduced motion:** at T_W the form swaps to the receipt instantly; the mark renders full-size and static; sentence and actions are present immediately; the standing-screen highlight is omitted (the closure sentence carries the change); every announcement is identical. Total active motion in the normal path: 420ms, composed of segments ≤200ms each.

## 5. Forbidden motion register

| Forbidden pattern | Reason |
|---|---|
| Bounce / overshoot / elastic settle | Imparts playfulness and physical chaos to a financially serious product; every Micro curve is specified with zero overshoot; overshoot on amounts would visually "guess" at a number — intolerable for recorded money. |
| Confetti / celebration particles / success sound-visuals | Success is closure, not entertainment; the 12-state vocabulary defines success as a non-blocking closure sentence; celebrating routine daily writes is patronizing by the tenth occurrence. |
| Animated counters (digits counting up/down) | Money numbers are final, exact, recorded facts; counting implies estimation and liveness that misrepresents the write; also illegible for exactly the duration that matters. |
| Excessive parallax / decorative depth | Depth that carries no location meaning is ornament (C-17); disorients scanning in RTL; costs frame budget on the low-end small-portrait devices that are Micro's real hardware. |
| Neon glow / pulsing glows / luminous focus effects | Off-palette and outside the measured role system (C-01–C-06); glow reads as marketing chrome, destroying the calm-professional target feel. |
| Entertainment-style motion (falling coins, piggy banks, mascots, badges) | Reduces trust in a tool that guards the owner's livelihood; the target feel is "financially serious but emotionally comfortable" — mascots break the first half. |
| Spinners or pulses over content where a truthful state exists | A direct lie about the screen's knowledge; violates honest-states discipline (C-06, C-11, C-12): existing content stays visible; unknown content gets skeletons + text; in-flight writes get button-level truth. |
| Shake / wiggle on error input (added) | Motion as punishment; errors are recovery events, not alarms; the input is preserved and the wording is calm. |
| Auto-advancing carousels / auto-playing motion (added) | Steals control from the owner; no owner action requested it; violates the focus principle (attention spent without consent). |
| Any travel transition > 280ms, or more than one simultaneous mover (added) | Exceeds the calm energy budget; multi-mover transitions split attention and break the one-primary-action discipline (C-14). |

## 6. Open questions for the orchestrator

1. **Haptics policy** — this report treats light haptics (keypad keypress, save completion) as optional and system-governed, outside the reduced-motion switch. Should Micro define a first-class haptics scale, or omit haptics entirely until tested on target devices?
2. **Dark-theme pressed mapping** — light pressed is fixed (`#b4613f`, press-only, C-04). For dark, this report proposes a `#332d27` 24% overlay over the resting background (a deliberate darkening, not an inversion). Does the color/semantics specialist report confirm this mapping, or should dark pressed be a fixed role value?
3. **Active-slot underline role** — proposed `#964e33` (light) / `#8fd5d6` (dark), the same role family as focus-visible. Confirm against the semantic-state distinctness matrix (C-06) so the position cursor, focus ring, and success mark never collide.
4. **Append-mode digit shift** — the entry model translates the digit run one slot per keypress (deterministic, frame fully stable) rather than zero digit movement. Confirm this satisfies the "stable digits" intent, or whether strict zero-movement entry (replace-only) should be prototyped and compared at 320px width.
5. **Skeleton truth for Finance first paint when an offline cache exists** — should cached figures render immediately with a staleness qualifier (honest-partial), with the sync indicator carrying the refresh, instead of a skeleton? This changes which waiting pattern owns that screen.
