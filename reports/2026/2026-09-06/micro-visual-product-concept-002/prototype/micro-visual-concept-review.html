What to inspect first

  • 1. Home: one question, one hero rhythm — today rows with action words, ruled facts grid (not cards), «—» where money is unknown.
  • 2. Finance: the 34px figure answers «وين الكاش؟» before anything else; deposit/amanah honesty lines.
  • 3. سجّل sheet → تسجيل بيع: NumericSurface — stable digits, position cursor, keypad + quick chips, then quiet completion (no counter, no confetti).
  • 4. Statement «كشف الأسبوع»: week chart with an unrecorded day rendered as a gap, plus the interpretation sentence under every chart.
  • 5. Toggle EN LTR: chevrons and the chart mirror; money and dates never mirror. Toggle dark: warm hairlines keep it from going cold.
  • 6. Width 320: rows, facts cells, and long Arabic labels must all survive — this was built and checked first.

The 12 states — where to see each

  • empty — Orders: search a word with no match.
  • loading — Statement: first-paint demo (genuinely-unknown case only).
  • saving — سجّل sale: button-level «جارٍ التسجيل…», screen stays truthful.
  • success — the receipt: closure sentence with final numbers + quiet mark.
  • error — failure demo: input preserved, nothing navigates.
  • offline — sync demo: truth line, writes stay enabled.
  • unknown — «—» in facts/amounts; «غير محدد بعد» roads.
  • partial — profit «تقديري» with bounded-gap sentence; locality/staleness line.
  • unsaved — discard guard when the sheet closes with a typed amount.
  • cancel — sheet drag/✕; dialogs «إلغاء».
  • back — Statement reader's رجوع.
  • correction — تراجع → معاينة الأثر (will-change/won't-change) → documented receipt note on Home.
